### 1.4 · AGENTE: `execution_governor`
**Arquivo:** `agents/execution-governor.agent.ts`

#### Modos de Execução
| Modo | Budget | Créditos | Agentes excluídos |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QUICK** | < R$5.000 — flash_sale/remarketing/upsell | 45–80 | strategic_doctrine, traffic_intelligence, ux_simplification |
| **STANDARD** | R$5.000–R$50.000 | 80–250 | — inclui strategic_doctrine, financial_projector, business_intelligence |

#### Classificação de Agentes
- **CORE:** command, profile_builder, strategy, strategic_core, campaign_memory
- **CONTEXTUAL:** strategic_doctrine, offer, financial_projector, traffic_intelligence, manager_agents
- **EXPENSIVE:** loops de critique
- **PASSIVE:** memory_compression, ux_simplification, business_intelligence, launch_debriefing

#### Regras
- Traffic Intelligence: só ativa se `hasTraffic=true`
- Mesma função → manter o mais preciso
- Business Intelligence: sempre passive
