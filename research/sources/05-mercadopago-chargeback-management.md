\# How to manage and prevent chargebacks This documentation will provide all the necessary information for managing and preventing chargebacks. ## What is a chargeback? A chargeback is generated when a \*\*customer does not recognize the charge on their debit or credit card.\*\* When this happens, the amount paid is withheld until the problem is resolved with the card-issuing institution. > NOTE > > For more information on what to do when you receive a chargeback, see \[our help page\](https://www.mercadopago.com.br/ajuda/reclamacao-comprador\_583).
