07

Advanced Modules in Production

7.1 — UTM Intelligence

Auto-reads UTMs from body + query params. Structured storage per contact. Conversion analytics by source/medium/campaign. Top sources ranked by volume and conversion.

7.2 — Viral Loop / Referral

Each captured lead receives a unique 8-char alphanumeric code. ?ref=CODE tracks referrals. Real-time referral counter. Top referrers in analytics. Native gamification.

7.3 — Send Time Optimization

Each contact's engagement hour recorded (open → UTC hour). Mode of hours → preferredSendHour per contact. Messages dispatched at each person's optimal time.

7.4 — Creative Fatigue Detector

Auto-detector: if CTR drops below 70% of historical peak and peak > 0.5%, alert "Creative fatigue detected" + refresh recommendation. Zero manual configuration.

7.5 — Server-Side Events (CAPI + TikTok)

Public endpoints for server-side event firing: Lead, Purchase, PageView, AddToCart. SHA-256 PII hashing before transmission. Meta Graph API v20 + TikTok Business API v1.3. LGPD-safe by design.

7.6 — LGPD Audit Trail

Every lead capture records: ISO consent timestamp, capture IP, exact consent text, UTM source, user agent. Auditable row in audit_logs. Full compliance with LGPD Art. 7 and Art. 9.

7.7 — Clone Studio

Clone recording session with face detection, automatic session reconnection, persistent cloneSessionId. Two video styles: With Clone (on-camera presence) and Faceless (narration/animation).

7.8 — Daily Video

Professional daily script generation for any social video format. Script + hook + captions + CTA + hashtags + ready upload instructions. 8–20 credits based on real market cost. TXT download for recording.

7.9 — AI Sales Team

5 specialist agents by funnel stage. Conversation kanban by stage. Persistent message history. "AI Suggestion" button generates instant contextual response. Integrated into main dashboard.

7.10 — Full Identification + LGPD

Dedicated Settings tab: CPF, CNPJ, corporate name, trade name, address with ViaCEP auto-fill. Three LGPD consents with auditable timestamps. Data Subject Rights (Art. 18): access, portability, deletion via pre-formatted email.

7.11 — NexOS Academy

Integrated training portal (nexos-academy). Professor Allan — AI agent specialized in NexOS methodology. Contextual lessons by launch track. Expanded token limit (2048), prompts with 10 pedagogical directives, real Brazilian examples. Bonus included in NexOS AI plans.

Each campaign generates proprietary data: winning creatives, winning audiences, objections, timing, regions, CPL, CPA, ROAS, conversion and retention. No competitor will have this Brazilian launch dataset.

Tools generate assets. NexOS executes. Execution creates dependency. The more the customer executes campaigns through NexOS, the harder it is to migrate — campaign history, audiences, sequences, and data remain on the platform.

14.4 — Proof Moat

The documented self-launch becomes the central case and public proof. Self-Proof Engine in production. The platform that sells itself with its own technology is the strongest argument for the prospect — they live the demonstration before buying.

14.5 — Category Moat

NexOS creates the category Growth Execution Infrastructure. Category creators tend to capture market leadership perception — and perception leadership precedes revenue leadership.

14.6 — Compliance Moat New

Native LGPD, CAPI server-side, audit trail by design. Competing platforms add compliance as an afterthought. In NexOS, compliance is architectural — reducing regulatory risk for the customer and the platform.
