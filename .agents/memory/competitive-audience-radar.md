---
name: Competitive Audience Radar
description: Defines compliant collection and activation of public positive audience signals around competitors.
---

Regional competitive intelligence includes monitoring authorized public social sources for positive audience interactions around direct, indirect, and substitute competitors. Signals become tenant-scoped audience opportunities and segments, not automatically contactable leads.

**Why:** Public engagement reveals demand, language, objections, advocates, and underserved audiences, but treating every interacting person as a sales lead would create privacy, platform-policy, consent, and reputational risk.

**How to apply:** Store source, public account reference, interaction context, timestamp, sentiment/intent, confidence, region inference provenance, and contact-permission status. Avoid sensitive/private data and unauthorized scraping; outreach requires a lawful basis, platform permission, frequency limits, opt-out, and explicit activation policy.
