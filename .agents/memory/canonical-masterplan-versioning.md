---
name: Canonical Masterplan Versioning
description: Governs how approved campaign plans become the authority for autonomous execution.
---

The Masterplan is a versioned, immutable campaign authority rather than a loose combination of strategy outputs. New material decisions create a new version; approving one supersedes the prior approved version.

**Why:** Autonomous agents and executors can otherwise act on different snapshots, making approval, evidence, recovery, and readiness claims unreliable.

**How to apply:** Bind campaign context, authorization gates, action evidence, and readiness scoring to the approved Masterplan version and fingerprint. Preserve explicit legacy fallback for campaigns created before versioning.
