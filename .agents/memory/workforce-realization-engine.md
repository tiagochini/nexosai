---
name: Workforce realization engine
description: Product doctrine for turning the Master Plan into a complete, connected and operating commercial system.
---

Realization is a first-class NexOS layer, not a minor content step. It is cumulative with the existing campaign, creative, sequence, social, WhatsApp, reporting and scheduler flows; it must connect and strengthen them, never replace or silently omit them. After Master Plan approval, the Workforce must build and connect the website, identity, pages, forms, tracking, domain, offer, funnel, checkout, communications, CRM, retargeting, onboarding, retention, reactivation, referral and authorized communities.

**Why:** The user corrected workflows that first underrepresented execution and later treated the new model as a replacement. NexOS creates, publishes, tests, measures and improves the system while preserving every previously defined operational capability.

**How to apply:** Use the two-axis Capability Index: 12 lifecycle stages describe what NexOS operates, while 12 maturity levels describe how each capability becomes observable, approvable, executable and production-proven. Every roadmap change must name its capability ID, lifecycle stage, maturity transition, preserved prior flow and evidence.
