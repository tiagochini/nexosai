---
name: NexOS Autonomous Agency Doctrine
description: Durable product doctrine for NexOS as an operational autonomous marketing agency, including workspace isolation, service layers, autonomy and cost principles.
---

NexOS is intended to replace a complete marketing, advertising and digital-launch agency, not act as a chatbot. Agents must plan, use tools, execute authorized actions, verify external results, recover failures and continuously optimize.

**Why:** The product must deliver real operational outcomes across launches, ongoing social media and market intelligence, while serving individuals, companies, agencies and other organizations globally.

**How to apply:** Treat each product or service as an isolated workspace with its own CEO/orchestrator, context and agent teams. Support three commercial/operational layers: launch execution, ongoing tiered social-media management, and a market-intelligence team. Multiple social accounts receive separate profiles and briefs. After final campaign approval, execution continues autonomously within the approved Masterplan and budget envelope; material changes to identity, Masterplan or external commitments require user initiative or explicit authorization. Optimize costs through context reuse, deterministic operations and model routing, never by making agents context-blind.

Commercial direction: initial market is Brazil but architecture is global from day one. Current intended onboarding fee is R$3,990, followed by credit or tier packages. Target gross margin is 85–99%, subject to separating variable third-party spend and validating unit economics before public launch.
