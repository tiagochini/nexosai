---
name: Radar commercial packaging
description: Commercial principles for pricing, activating and protecting margin on NexOS competitive intelligence.
---

Sell Radar by operational intensity and business value, not by adding a small markup to model tokens. Packages must bound campaigns, competitors, regions, scan cadence, Council usage and retention; high-frequency launch monitoring belongs in a time-limited War Room.

Commercial activation must fail honestly. Until the existing billing provider supports arbitrary add-ons safely, a customer action creates a pending commercial request, not payment success or an active entitlement.

**Why:** Public-search and Council costs vary with frequency and volume. Central capacity limits protect gross margin, while premium value comes from continuous evidence-backed intelligence and coordinated action rather than raw token cost.

**How to apply:** Reserve usage atomically before cost-generating work, keep readable historical intelligence available after expiry, never automate third-party interaction, and require explicit authorized activation after commercial confirmation. Prices may be adjusted centrally without weakening entitlement enforcement.
