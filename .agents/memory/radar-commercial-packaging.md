---
name: Radar commercial packaging
description: Commercial principles for pricing, activating and protecting margin on NexOS competitive intelligence.
---

Sell Radar by operational intensity and business value, not by adding a small markup to model tokens. Packages must bound campaigns, competitors, regions, scan cadence, Council usage and retention; high-frequency launch monitoring belongs in a time-limited War Room.

Paid NexOS subscriptions include Radar Pro for 90 calendar days from the later of confirmed subscription payment or the Radar commercial launch date. Afterward, the customer explicitly chooses Essential, Pro, Scale or no Radar; War Room remains a separate campaign-scoped 30-day add-on.

BRL packages use direct Asaas PIX/Boleto checkout and activate only after the payment status is verified with Asaas. USD remains a commercial-request path until an international settlement provider is integrated.

**Why:** Public-search and Council costs vary with frequency and volume. Central capacity limits protect gross margin, while premium value comes from continuous evidence-backed intelligence and coordinated action rather than raw token cost.

**How to apply:** Reserve usage atomically before cost-generating work, keep readable historical intelligence after expiry or a no-Radar choice, verify campaign ownership for War Room, and never activate from an unverified webhook payload. Prices may be adjusted centrally without weakening entitlement enforcement.
