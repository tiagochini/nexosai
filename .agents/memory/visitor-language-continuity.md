---
name: Visitor language continuity
description: Why language selection on the public site must carry into the application.
---

Treat the institutional home at `/`, the separate pre-launch page at `/landing/`, and app login as one visitor journey for Portuguese, English and Spanish. A language selected before login should carry across those surfaces; once signed in, the account's saved locale remains authoritative. Australian English can use the English public copy.

**Why:** A visible language selector on one NexOS-branded page does not help visitors who are actually viewing another. A request for the "main page" was initially applied to the pre-launch page while the user's screenshot showed the institutional home at `/`.

**How to apply:** Match the user's screenshot or copy to its actual route before editing a public page. Public routes should honor the visitor's shared preference and preserve older landing preferences when possible. Keep language labels easy to find on small screens, update the document language, and localize interface copy without altering campaign, user or agent-generated content.
