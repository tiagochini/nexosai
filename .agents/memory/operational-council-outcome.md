---
name: Operational Council outcome boundary
description: Why the Council's later-cycle verification cannot be treated as provider authorization or business KPI proof.
---

An operational outcome belongs to a later Council cycle with recorded minutes citing the exact governed action's realization contract. Verification is an observation of independent M09 receipt, readback, approved QC and monitoring for the latest attempt; an inconclusive observation stays immutable but may be followed by a new observation when evidence changes.

**Why:** A meeting decision cannot authorize an external mutation, and a provider-confirmed operation does not by itself prove a commercial KPI was achieved. Without an explicit later review, a one-time status check would be misrepresented as a closed operational loop.

**How to apply:** Extend Council actions only after the new family has a governed realization contract; retain exact Master Plan binding and later-cycle minutes, and keep independent commercial performance measurement separate from execution confirmation.
