---
name: Progressive capability navigation
description: Product rule for exposing autonomous-agent capabilities in the customer interface.
---

Add customer-facing sidebar areas such as CRM, SEO and Schedule progressively, only after the corresponding agents can execute the underlying tasks reliably. Do not add all future areas at once, disabled buttons, or empty “coming soon” pages.

**Why:** The user wants customers to see the volume and value of work NexOS performs for them, even when they do not operate the tools manually. Visible outputs make the autonomous operation concrete without turning NexOS back into a manual tool suite.

**How to apply:** When a capability passes its execution and evidence gates, add its navigation entry and a primarily observational workspace: results, activity timeline, decisions, reports, exports and any required approvals. Keep internal certification/readiness controls hidden; expose customer-relevant work and outcomes instead. Existing areas remain absent until they are functional.
