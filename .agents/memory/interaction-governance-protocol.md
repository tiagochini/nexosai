---
name: Interaction Governance Protocol
description: Durable operating rules for AI-assisted social interaction based on competitive audience signals.
---

Interactions on third-party content are assisted by default. AI may observe, score, explain and draft, but a deterministic governor decides whether the action may proceed. Automatic execution requires a verified official adapter capability and an owned asset or an authorized conversation.

**Why:** Public visibility does not make a person freely contactable, and spreading activity across several connected accounts must never be used to evade platform limits or increase spam volume.

**How to apply:** Enforce workspace-wide quotas before account quotas, deduplicate recipients across every account, honor global cooldown and opt-out, require evidence and context, cap CTA intensity, and fail closed on missing capability, unhealthy connections, high risk, absent contact permission or unsupported ownership. Council drafts should contribute one specific contextual insight in a natural, varied voice, without exposing competitive surveillance or disguising a sales pitch. Revalidate the final edited text at approval time so human modification cannot bypass safety, similarity, CTA or channel-migration rules.
