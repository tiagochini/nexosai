---
name: Audiovisual Council and Trailers
description: Operating contract for collaborative production planning and derived trailer deliverables.
---

Audiovisual specialists propose and critique in explicit rounds; the director
synthesizes a schema-validated executable plan. Only that synthesis may compile
scene prompts, source modes and timeline instructions used by providers and
renderers.

**Why:** Persisting intelligent recommendations without compiling them into media
instructions makes agents appear operational while generation still follows an
older storyboard. A director synthesis creates one auditable source of truth.

**How to apply:** Persist every member status/error and immutable council version.
Synthetic scenes must use compiled prompts and cannot silently become avatar
scenes. Optional 15s/30s trailers apply only to masters over 30 seconds, use their
own approved segment plan/render job/output lineage, and must pass exact-duration
verification rather than trimming the master opening.
