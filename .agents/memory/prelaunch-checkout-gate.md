---
name: Pre-launch checkout gate
description: Commercial rule for the NexOS landing funnel and when public checkout may be exposed.
---

The public NexOS landing must operate as a structured, ethical PLF-style pre-launch funnel until the complete product is ready for launch. Its conversion goal is lead capture, priority-list enrollment and diagnostic/pre-launch content—not payment.

**Why:** The public checkout must not be exposed before NexOS is online with the complete, governed Meta Ads integration. Building demand before operational readiness is intentional, but pretending that purchase or immediate activation is available would break trust.

**How to apply:** Keep purchase CTAs and checkout unavailable on the public landing until the Meta Ads launch gate is explicitly cleared. Use truthful anticipation, objection handling and repeated lead-capture CTAs without fabricated deadlines, scarcity, testimonials or guaranteed outcomes.

The landing must lead with the buyer's expensive coordination problem and the operational transformation, not with agent counts, architecture or governance. Its memorable commercial thesis is that companies should stop hiring disconnected parts to execute one strategy; NexOS carries one market decision through the full commercial lifecycle without losing context.

**Why:** A visually strong but architecture-led version was rejected as weak in hooks, content and sales logic. Competitor research showed that effective category leaders sell a concrete replacement for the buyer's current reality before explaining the technology.

**How to apply:** Treat “64 agents” as supporting proof. Use concrete before/after scenes, verbs and outputs; establish the mechanism before the module catalog or future pricing; keep technical controls late as trust and risk reduction.
